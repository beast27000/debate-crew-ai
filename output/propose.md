Strict laws to regulate LLMs are crucial in preventing their misuse. AI can be used for malicious purposes such as data poisoning, deepfakes, or generating convincing phishing emails that trick users into divulging sensitive information. These threats need legal regulation. Moreover, LLMs can be used to create autonomous weapons systems that pose significant security concerns. Effective governance through legislation is necessary to mitigate these risks. This should include strict data protection laws, liability for harm caused by AI systems, and provisions for responsible AI development. By establishing regulatory frameworks, we can ensure the safe deployment of AI and prevent it from being misused, thereby safeguarding our society.
